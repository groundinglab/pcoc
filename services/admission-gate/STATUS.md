## Not on the website wire

`provisional`, `held`, `revoked`, and `banned` are admission states. They are not tokens in the locked §15.1 website table, so this build does not add them to `sign-in.json`. The website keeps `pending` while admission is unsettled, `accepted` on a thorough accept, `rejected` on a refuse or a revoke, and `too_few_waypoints_anchored` for that settled shortfall.

`POST /v1/sign-in` returns after the fast checks. When those checks hand the attempt to admission the body is `{"delivery":"ok"}`, and the handler does not wait for thorough. Thorough runs in the background. A fake leg revokes the grant and bans the key. A thorough accept writes the friendly entry. Site UX should treat that delivery-ok response, and a provisional grant that has not settled, as signed in immediately. The result token stays `pending` until thorough settles. That reading is for the site. This service does not call the site and adds no wire field (#127).
