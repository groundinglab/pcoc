# Apps

Demonstrators. They are not part of the specification. A producer or a verifier built from `spec/` and `spec/conformance/` does not need them.

| App | What it is |
|---|---|
| [desktop](desktop/) | PCOC Hike for Linux. Makes, checks, and signs in on the machine in front of you, using the Rust reference. |
| [android](android/) | A fork of the Primal Android app. It can make a PCOC, check one locally, and sign in. The sign-in decision belongs to the Gate. |
