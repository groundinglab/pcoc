# Where does the trouble start?

Most harms in open systems trace back to issuance.

Spam, bots, scripted floods, swarms of fake accounts. A gatekeeper who can switch you off. Moderation that turns on someone's opinion. Each looks like its own problem, with its own fix. Look one step back and most share a root: how the identifier was handed out in the first place.

There are two usual answers. The first is free and unlimited. Anyone can make an address or a key, as many as they like, for nothing. That is open, and it floods. A thousand accounts cost what one costs. The second is issued by a third party. A registrar, a platform, a phone-number check. That limits the flood, and it installs an office. [Issued, listed, or made?](issued-listed-or-made.md) The office decides who gets one, can take it back, and has to be trusted to keep deciding fairly.

Policy is the usual repair, and it sits downstream. Filters, reputation, reporting, review. Each is a rule applied after the identifiers already exist. When making one is free, the rule faces new arrivals faster than it can judge them. When the rule tightens, it needs a judge, and the judge is a controller. Policy does not fix cheap issuance. It adds a second office on top of the first.

The other place to act is the issuance itself. Let anyone issue their own, with no office to ask. Put the cost into the making, so each object carries what it took to produce. That idea is old: Dwork and Naor priced a message in computation in 1992, and Adam Back's Hashcash did it for email in 1997. [How do you make a digital thing scarce?](how-do-you-make-a-digital-thing-scarce.md) The making holds memory between public ticks, so it cannot start before the tick it reads, and the design aims to give bulk making no discount. [What kind of cost refuses to divide?](what-kind-of-cost-refuses-to-divide.md) The limit is on making, not on permission.

The claim is not that you cannot make many. It is that many costs many times what one costs. It does not prove a person. Measurement and policy stay separate: the object says what was made, and whether to accept it is a gate each holder keeps. [Why is the check always cheaper than the making?](why-can-saying-no-be-cheap-when-saying-yes-cannot.md)
