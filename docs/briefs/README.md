# Briefs

Eleven short pages. One question each. They teach the ideas. They do not amend the specification, and they do not invent a check.

Read in any order. The order below is the suggested order.

1. [Where does the trouble start?](where-does-the-trouble-start.md) - Most harms in open systems trace back to issuance.
2. [How do you make a digital thing scarce?](how-do-you-make-a-digital-thing-scarce.md) - Cost goes in the object, not in a door.
3. [Issued, listed, or made?](issued-listed-or-made.md) - A cap and a list are the same office. A made object has no office.
4. [What kind of cost refuses to divide?](what-kind-of-cost-refuses-to-divide.md) - The cost does not amortise. Two objects need two terrains.
5. [Why memory, and why a clock?](why-memory-and-why-a-clock.md) - Occupancy is the unit. The time half is not in the bytes.
6. [What does a block fix?](what-does-a-block-fix.md) - Data has a where. A block also has a when.
7. [How do you prove when the work happened?](how-do-you-prove-when-the-work-happened.md) - Effort has no when. A public sequence supplies one.
8. [Can you check a stranger without asking anyone?](can-you-check-a-stranger-without-asking.md) - The evidence is in the file, not in a lookup.
9. [Why is the check always cheaper than the making?](why-can-saying-no-be-cheap-when-saying-yes-cannot.md) - Every depth of check costs the verifier less than the making cost the producer.
10. [What follows from two things?](what-follows-from-two-things.md) - The object is already a composition. The pattern is the repetition. The uses are a guess.
11. [Why carry the cost forward?](why-carry-the-cost-forward.md) - The finished file is a spent commitment the holder can put at risk. Keeping it is optional.

A page may name a sibling in one sentence. It does not repay that sibling's argument.
