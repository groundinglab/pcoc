# Operational history versus proposals

Snapshot dated 2026-11-08. This is a factual status extraction, **not completion or validation of the v0.5.3 experiment**. That experiment and any further implementation/testing remain outside this documentation release.

- Playbook v0.5.3 is in operational testing. Its running body is internal r6c; the stamped predecessor was r6b, different bytes. No producer-ticket stamp is claimed; its launch was a one-off override, not a changed general rule.
- Process policy v0.5.2 remains the active operational policy. Publishing a playbook did not adopt proposed keys or enable automatic merge.
- Existing mechanical ticket checks are partial. The original standalone r2 template and later TC-heading harness have known drift; do not present them as a synchronized general validator.
- Specified later checks and launcher/host/template-sync automation are not all built. A manual procedure can still be documented and used honestly.
- Corrected v0.5.4 text/harness diffs and ticket-stamping recipe are **prepared proposals**, not assembled, regression-tested or adopted by this release. They address template authority, accessible verbatim evidence, diagnosis/falsifier before retry and scan scope. No active run or policy is modified, and this WIP does not wait for them.

The original full playbook, operational policy, historical template/scripts and private review records are not included verbatim: redistribution rights and private-history cleanup are unresolved. The [inventory](../sources/README.md) names every pertinent source and its disposition. This is a deliberately partial, original manual kit—not an assertion that all internal tooling has been published.
